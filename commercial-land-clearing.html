Commercial Land Clearing for Developers and Builders

Commercial clearing is a different job than residential lot work — the scale is larger, the timeline is tighter, and the consequences of delays ripple through the whole project schedule. We understand that and we work accordingly. When a developer needs a site cleared before their excavator arrives, we're not the variable that pushes the schedule back.

We handle commercial land clearing across Licking, Muskingum, Knox, and Fairfield counties — development sites, shopping center expansions, industrial park clearing, warehouse and distribution facility sites, and municipal projects. Tracked forestry mulching equipment handles multi-acre sites efficiently and leaves the land in better condition than dozer clearing for most project types.

What Commercial Clearing Involves

  • Multi-acre development site clearing for residential subdivisions
  • Commercial and retail site clearing
  • Industrial and warehouse site preparation
  • Municipal and county property clearing
  • Solar farm site preparation
  • Pipeline and utility corridor clearing
  • Large-scale invasive species removal on commercial properties

For commercial projects, we provide detailed written proposals with per-acre or flat-rate pricing, a defined scope of work, and a realistic project timeline. We can work in phases to align with your construction schedule, and we communicate proactively if anything on site changes the scope.

"We cleared a 22-acre commercial development site near Heath in Licking County — a mixed-use development that's been in the planning stages for a while. The site had about 15 years of pioneer growth on it, mostly cottonwood, box elder, and a heavy honeysuckle understory. We phased the work over four days and had the site ready for their civil engineer's site visit on schedule. The GC told us later it was the smoothest part of the whole pre-construction phase."

Working With Your Project Team

Commercial projects involve multiple stakeholders — developers, GCs, civil engineers, municipalities. We're used to that environment. We can coordinate with your project manager, work to survey stakes and setback requirements, and provide documentation of work completed if your project requires it.

If your site has environmental considerations — wetlands, stream buffers, protected tree areas — we work to those boundaries. We're not the ones who will determine what's required, but we will respect the specs you give us and flag anything on site that looks like it might need attention from your environmental consultant.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you price commercial clearing?

Most commercial jobs are flat-rated after a site walk — per-acre pricing on a defined scope of work. For large or phased projects, we can structure pricing by phase. We provide a written proposal with clear scope, pricing, and timeline.

Commercial and multi-acre projects often have permit requirements that residential jobs don't — see our guide on land clearing permits in Ohio before scheduling.

Can you work to survey stakes and setback requirements?

Yes. We ask for the survey and any environmental constraints before we start. We work to the boundaries you define and flag anything that looks inconsistent with our instructions.

How quickly can you mobilize?

For commercial projects, we typically schedule within two to three weeks of proposal acceptance. For urgent situations, call us directly — we can often work faster depending on our current schedule.

Do you do phased clearing?

Yes. For large sites where construction is phased, we can clear in sections aligned with your build sequence rather than clearing the whole site at once. That keeps earlier-cleared areas from reverting to brush before construction reaches them.
