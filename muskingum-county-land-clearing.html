Land Clearing in Muskingum County

Muskingum County is different terrain than Licking County to the west. The land here is hillier — dissected by the Muskingum River and its tributaries, with steep wooded ridges and narrow valley bottoms that characterize the Appalachian plateau transition zone. If you've driven south of Zanesville on US-40 or out toward Blue Rock State Forest, you know what we're talking about. The topography shapes every land clearing decision we make on a Muskingum County property.

That terrain is exactly why we run tracked equipment. A wheeled machine on a steep wooded hillside in Muskingum County is a liability — poor traction, soil damage on slopes, and limited access to the areas that actually need clearing. Our tracked forestry mulcher handles grade changes that wheeled equipment can't approach, and does it without tearing up the ground in the process.

The clay and shale soils common in Muskingum County make erosion a real concern on any disturbed slope. Forestry mulching addresses that directly — the mulch layer left on site provides immediate erosion control on slopes where bulldozing would leave bare, vulnerable subsoil.

What Land Clearing Looks Like in Muskingum County

The land clearing work we do in Muskingum County is a mix of rural residential, agricultural, and recreational property. The county has a lot of acreage that was farmed hard in the mid-20th century and has reverted to brush and second-growth timber as farms consolidated. We see a lot of pasture reclamation work — land that was open within living memory and has grown back into woodland or heavy brush.

Recreational property is also a category here. Hunting properties, weekend land, and rural retreats where owners want to manage the property — create food plots, open up view corridors, reclaim old field edges — make up a meaningful share of our Muskingum County work.

Around Zanesville itself, we handle residential lot clearing for new construction and commercial site clearing for development projects along the major corridors.

"One of the more challenging jobs we've done in Muskingum County was a 9-acre hillside property outside Dresden. The owner had bought it as a hunting property and wanted the old overgrown orchard reclaimed and a view corridor opened up toward the valley. The grade was significant in sections — steep enough that a skid steer would have had real trouble. The tracked machine handled it without issue. We opened up the orchard, cleared the corridor, and left the property in better shape than we found it."

Frequently Asked Questions — Muskingum County

Can you clear steep hillside properties in Muskingum County?

Yes — it's one of our specialties in this region. Tracked equipment handles grades that wheeled machines can't safely access. We assess slope and access at the estimate and are upfront if any section of a property is beyond safe operating limits.

What invasive species are most common in Muskingum County?

Bush honeysuckle, multiflora rose, and autumn olive are widespread across Muskingum County woodlots and field edges. Ailanthus is common along road cuts and disturbed areas, particularly in the Zanesville area. All are effectively handled by forestry mulching.

Do you do hunting property management clearing?

Yes. Food plot clearing, view corridor opening, bedding area management, and old field edge reclamation are all work we do on recreational properties in Muskingum County. We work to your management plan or discuss options with you at the estimate.

How far from Zanesville do you travel?

We cover all of Muskingum County regardless of location. Rural township properties in the southern and eastern parts of the county are not a problem — we factor drive time into scheduling but not pricing.
